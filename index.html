About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, advised by Prof. Debdeep Mukhopadhyay and Prof. Satrajit Ghosh, and I collaborate closely with Dr. Sikhar Patranabis at IBM Research India. My PhD is supported by a Google PhD Fellowship (2021) in Security, Cryptography, and Privacy. I expect to defend my thesis in November 2026.

My research focuses on applied cryptography, with an emphasis on secure computation and privacy-preserving systems. I develop efficient cryptographic primitives, particularly Function Secret Sharing (FSS), Distributed Point Functions (DPFs), and related constructions, to improve the scalability and communication efficiency of secure protocols. My work spans threshold cryptography, encrypted search, private machine learning, and secure distributed computation, combining rigorous security analysis under standard cryptographic assumptions with practical implementations and experimental evaluation. I also investigate implementation security, including reverse firewalls, hardware Trojans, and side-channel resilience, with the goal of building cryptographic systems that are both provably secure and practically deployable.

Before joining IIT Kharagpur, I received my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Guwahati in 2020.
