Chandan Kumar

PhD candidate, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Kharagpur

I am a PhD candidate at IIT Kharagpur, advised by Prof. Debdeep Mukhopadhyay and Prof. Satrajit Ghosh, and I collaborate closely with Dr. Sikhar Patranabis at IBM Research India. My PhD is supported by a Google PhD Fellowship (2021) in Security, Cryptography, and Privacy. I expect to defend my thesis in November 2026.

My research is in applied cryptography, focused on privacy-enhancing protocols with security proofs and working implementations. Most of it builds on function secret sharing and distributed point functions, which I use to construct encrypted search (ThORY, MUSSE), threshold protocols, and private machine learning inference. A second line of work looks at implementation security, including cryptographic reverse firewalls—our ESORICS 2025 paper TERRA constructs a reverse firewall that remains secure in the presence of hardware Trojans—and side-channel analysis. I work best on problems where a protocol has to survive contact with a real system.

Before joining IIT Kharagpur, I received my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Guwahati in 2020.
