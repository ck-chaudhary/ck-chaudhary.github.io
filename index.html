Chandan Kumar

PhD candidate, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Kharagpur

I am a PhD candidate at IIT Kharagpur, advised by Prof. Debdeep Mukhopadhyay and Prof. Satrajit Ghosh. I also work closely with Dr. Sikhar Patranabis at IBM Research India. My PhD is supported by a Google PhD Fellowship (2021) in Security, Privacy and Cryptography.

I work on cryptographic protocols for distributed settings. Much of my recent work builds on function secret sharing (FSS): compact-key FSS constructions, threshold searchable encryption (ThORY, MUSSE), and FSS-based privacy-preserving machine learning. I am also interested in cryptographic reverse firewalls—our ESORICS 2025 paper TERRA constructs a reverse firewall that remains secure in the presence of hardware Trojans—and in hardware security topics such as PUFs and electronic supply-chain protection.

Before joining IIT Kharagpur, I received my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Guwahati in 2020.
