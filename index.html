PhD Candidate · Cryptography & Security

Chandan Kumar

Google PhD Fellow 2021 IIT Kharagpur · SEAL Lab IACR Member

I design cryptographic primitives and build systems that close the gap between theoretical MPC and real-world deployment. My primary focus is Function Secret Sharing (FSS): compact-key constructions, threshold FSS-based encrypted search, and FSS-powered privacy-preserving ML. I also work on Trojan-resilient reverse firewalls for cryptographic applications.

What I work on

Research Areas

⚙️

Function Secret Sharing

Compact-key FSS with non-linear decoders. Threshold DPFs. Silent, communication-efficient distributed computation.

🔍

Searchable Encryption

Threshold encrypted search (ThORY). Multi-server conjunctive SSE with sub-linear complexity and formal leakage bounds (MUSSE).

🤖

Privacy-Preserving ML

FSS-based secure ML inference. Access-controlled PPML with attribute-based gates. Sub-linear inference costs.

🛡️

Reverse Firewalls

Trojan-resilient RF in the PC-III hardware model (TERRA). Side-channel and covert-channel analysis of RF implementations.

🔐

MPC & Secure Computation

Lightweight MPC from FSS primitives. Threshold protocols. Connections to PCG/VOLE-based ZK systems.

💻

Hardware Security

Side-channel analysis on embedded systems (OP-TEE/TrustZone). EM-based CPA. Hardware Trojan threat modeling.
