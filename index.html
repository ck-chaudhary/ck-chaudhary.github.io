Google PhD Fellow 2021 · Security, Cryptography & Privacy

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, IIT Kharagpur, advised by Prof. Debdeep Mukhopadhyay and Prof. Satrajit Ghosh.

My research is in applied cryptography. The main thread is Function Secret Sharing (FSS) — a primitive that distributes computation across non-colluding servers — and its use in encrypted search, private ML inference, and multi-server protocols. Both the theory and the implementations matter to me.

A second line of work concerns reverse firewalls in hardware-adversarial settings. Our ESORICS 2025 paper TERRA formalises Trojan-resilient reverse firewalls in the PC-III circuit model and gives the first concrete instantiations with formal security proofs, addressing a gap left open by Mironov and Stephens-Davidowitz (2015).

I collaborate closely with Dr. Sikhar Patranabis at IBM Research India and with Dr. Suvradip Chakraborty at Visa Research USA.

Research areas

What I Work On

01 · FSS / HSS

Function Secret Sharing

Compact-key DPF and DCF constructions that reduce key size by replacing linear decoding with a non-linear decoder, without relaxing correctness. A second thread covers Threshold DPFs: point functions whose output is secret-shared across servers, enabling communication-efficient distributed computation.

02 · SSE

Encrypted Search

ThORY gives sub-linear search in the threshold SSE setting, built from Threshold DPFs with a seed-homomorphic PRG and Shamir secret sharing. MUSSE handles conjunctive queries across two servers via VDMPFs, with leakage provably below single-server schemes OXT and HXT. Both carry simulation-based security proofs.

03 · PPML

Privacy-Preserving ML

AC-PPML integrates attribute-based access control into FSS-based private ML inference without modifying the underlying inference protocol. Authorized parties recover the output; unauthorized parties see something computationally indistinguishable from uniform. The construction targets practical two-server deployments on GPU hardware.

04 · RF

Reverse Firewalls

TERRA formalises Trojan-resilient reverse firewalls in the PC-III circuit model and gives concrete instantiations for Coin Tossing and Oblivious Transfer, addressing a gap left open since Mironov and Stephens-Davidowitz (2015). Follow-up work (LEAK³) identifies a covert channel in PC-III's secret multiplication and proposes countermeasures validated on real hardware.

05 · FHE / MPC

Secure Computation

Threshold FHE with hardware acceleration (FACT) and MPC constructions built from FSS primitives. Also: an oblivious transfer protocol that avoids the physical transfer of a hardware root-of-trust, presented at ApPLIED @ PODC 2023.

06 · HW-SEC

Hardware Security

Correlation power analysis on OP-TEE/TrustZone (Raspberry Pi), circuit-level hardware Trojan threat modeling, HardCODE — circuit obfuscation via data encryption, and PUF-blockchain integration for electronic supply-chain security.

Compact Key Function Secret Sharing with Non-linear Decoder
Chandan Kumar, Sikhar Patranabis, Debdeep Mukhopadhyay
Constructs the most compact p-party FSS keys for point and comparison functions at the time of publication. The DPF achieves a 2n/2-factor key reduction over Boyle–Gilboa–Ishai (Eurocrypt 2015); the DCF reduces by qp−1. The gain trades linear decoding for a non-linear decoder.
