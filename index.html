Chandan Kumar

Cryptography & Secure Computation

I design cryptographic primitives at the intersection of theory and real systems. My core focus is Function Secret Sharing (FSS) — a lightweight primitive for distributing computation over untrusted servers — and its dual, Homomorphic Secret Sharing (HSS). I work on compact-key FSS constructions, threshold FSS-based encrypted search, and FSS-powered privacy-preserving ML inference. Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) is a concurrent thread, particularly threshold variants and their hardware acceleration.

A parallel line of work studies Trojan-resilient reverse firewalls — cryptographic wrappers that sanitize a party's outgoing messages to prevent subliminal exfiltration, even when the underlying hardware is malicious. My ESORICS 2025 paper, TERRA, is the first formally proven RF tolerating hardware Trojans in the PC-III circuit model. I am currently investigating how covert channels inside MPC gadgets can defeat even well-designed firewalls.

I did my B.Tech in CSE at IIIT Guwahati and grew up in Varanasi — a city that teaches patience with long problems. I hold the Google PhD Fellowship 2021 in Security, Cryptography and Privacy, and I collaborate closely with Dr. Sikhar Patranabis at IBM Research India.

What I work on

Research Areas

01 · FSS / HSS

Function Secret Sharing

Compact-key FSS with non-linear decoders. Threshold DPFs. Silent, communication-efficient distributed computation without interaction.

02 · SSE

Encrypted Search

Threshold encrypted search (ThORY). Multi-server conjunctive SSE with sub-linear complexity and formal leakage bounds (MUSSE).

03 · PPML

Privacy-Preserving ML

FSS-based secure inference. Access-controlled PPML with attribute-based policy gates. Sub-linear inference costs at deployment scale.

04 · RF

Reverse Firewalls

Trojan-resilient RF in the PC-III hardware model (TERRA). Covert-channel analysis of RF implementations over MPC gadgets.

05 · FHE / MPC

Secure Computation

Threshold FHE and its acceleration. Lightweight MPC from FSS primitives. Connections to VOLE-based ZK and PCG techniques.

06 · HW-SEC

Hardware Security

EM-based CPA on OP-TEE/TrustZone running on Raspberry Pi. Hardware Trojan threat modeling at the circuit level.
